Navigation system & view modes

The layer under the layouts: what stays fixed everywhere (the global frame), what adapts to context (the rail), how a calc opens from anywhere (peek → split → full), and how one calc wears different faces for different audiences (view modes). The rule behind all of it: one model, many lenses — never two primary objects on screen.

SYS 01

Anatomy of the global frame

Every screen in the product is this frame. Nothing else is ever global — no second toolbar, no floating menus. If a control isn’t one of these six, it lives inside the stage.

SYS 05

View modes — one model, four lenses

The calc is the data; the view is a lens picked per audience. Authors live in Notebook, spreadsheet folks get Sheet, recipients who shouldn’t see formulas get Form, and the internet gets a Widget. Same URL, same live engine — only the lens changes.

SYS 06

The URL is the state

No hidden app state: identity, your edits, and the lens all live in the address bar. Copying the URL always reproduces exactly what you’re looking at — which is why Share is just “copy”.

instacalc.com/kazad/party-budget#guests=45&view=form
IdentityAuthor + calc name. Stable forever; remixes get their own.
Your stateValues you changed, in the hash — private until you share the link, never hitting a server.
The lensnotebook · sheet · form · widget — the same calc, dressed for its audience.
Companion pages: the layout gallery · workspace user flows. The system rule, once more: one model, many lenses — and every state is a link.

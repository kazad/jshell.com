What this does

The stamp is the pill shape the counter learned from this photo — the same silhouette it counts with. Move the mouse: it lands the stamp under your cursor, spins it to the best angle, and reports how much of it falls on pill material.

Green outline = a stamp this good was counted nearby. Amber = good overlap but nothing counted here — that is a miss worth showing me.

Placing stamps: click the image to drop a stamp there. Drag its middle to move it, drag an end to rotate it onto the pill's axis. To delete: right-click it (or alt/shift-click, or the ✕ next to it in the list below). , . rotate the selected one 2°. Each placed stamp shows live overlap and whether the counter got that pill.

Rotating the cursor stamp: scroll the wheel over the image, drag the angle slider, or press S to snap the stamp to the pill edges under the cursor (works inside piles, where overlap alone cannot tell). [ ] nudge 2° · R back to auto · M mark.

Inside a dense pile every angle covers ~100% foreground, so AUTO picks arbitrarily — that is expected, and why manual exists. Your marked angle is what tells me the true pill axis.
